# Microsoft Store listing — Local AI 4 You (English, en-US)

Copy each block into the field with the same name in Partner Center → your app → Submission → Store listings → English (United States).

## Description

Find out which AI really runs on your computer, and use the best one.

Local AI 4 You looks at your graphics card, memory and processor and shows, in seconds, which local AI models fit your machine: from the smartest to the lightest, with the Ollama download command ready. No more downloading 20 GB only to find out it freezes.

RADAR
300+ models from Ollama and Hugging Face, updated daily, ranked for your hardware. The top 5 get a podium. Filter by use: chat, code, agents, data extraction, reasoning, images and long documents.

CALCULATOR
How much memory each model uses, how much context fits on your card and how fast it answers. Simulate a new graphics card and see if the upgrade is worth it before spending.

DUELS AND MEASUREMENTS
Torn between two models? The duel measures both on your computer, with the same questions from public tests (MMLU-Pro, IFEval, HumanEval, GSM8K and more), shows the margin of error and tells you when it's a tie.

DECIDE
See the best installed model for each use and which ones you can delete without losing anything.

FIRST STEPS
A 3-step guide: turn on Ollama, install your first model and run your first duel.

PRIVATE
Everything runs on your computer. No account, no sign-up. The app sends nothing unless you ask.

Free and open source. Available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Requires Ollama (free, ollama.com) to download and run models. Without it, Radar and the Calculator work normally.
