How this list is built

Only models that fit in memory at good compression quality make the list. The order favors the estimated intelligence of the model; speed counts until it is comfortable to read (about 10 tokens per second). Everything here is estimated from real file sizes, for Windows, 32 GB of RAM and a single card. To measure for real on your own computer, the free app runs duels between your installed models.

Common questions

What is the best AI model for the GTX 1660 Super 6 GB?

Today it is WeVisOne-VL-4B (Q5_K_M), which should generate about 81 tokens per second. For coding, the top pick is qwen3.5 4B.

Do I need internet or a subscription?

Only internet to download. After that the model runs on your computer with Ollama, a free program: no subscription, and your conversations are not sent anywhere.

How do I install it?

Install Ollama, open the terminal and paste the command shown next to the model. The download starts and, when it finishes, you can start chatting.
